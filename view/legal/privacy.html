Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to All in One. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our multi-social communication hub desktop application ("the App"). The App allows you to manage multiple social media accounts—including WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WeChat, and Telegram—within a single, unified interface.

2. Information We Collect

All in One is designed with a strong focus on privacy. We primarily process data on your local device.

A. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following information locally on your computer:

  • Account Configurations: Platform types, account display names, and local partition identifiers.
  • Session Data: Cookies, cache, and authentication tokens generated by the third-party platforms you log into.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as window sizes, selected platforms, and custom display layouts.

B. Synchronization Feature (Optional)

If you enable the Sync Sessions feature, the App uses your provided GitHub credentials (Personal Access Token) to securely back up your session data and configurations to your private GitHub repository. We do not have access to this synchronized data as it travels directly between your local device and your private repository.

C. Backend Cloud Services

For optional features like user registration, password resets, and license management, we collect basic registration data:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Hashed passwords

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage and isolate your sessions across different social media platforms securely.
  • To persist your active logins so you don't have to log in repeatedly.
  • To synchronize your settings across multiple devices using your private GitHub repository (if enabled).

4. Third-Party Platforms

The App acts as an embedded web browser that loads the official web interfaces of WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WeChat, and Telegram.

  • Your interactions, messages, and personal data on those platforms are governed strictly by the Privacy Policies of those respective services.
  • We do not intercept, read, or store the contents of your messages. Direct communication always happens between the third-party platform's servers and the web view handled by your computer.

5. Data Security & Isolation

  • Each of your messaging accounts runs in an isolated session partition. This ensures that cookies and local storage are kept entirely separate, preventing cross-contamination of sessions.
  • We strongly recommend securing your computer with strong passwords and up-to-date antivirus software to protect the local data stored by the App.

6. Managing and Deleting Your Data

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete any linked account directly within the App, which wipes the local session data for that account.
  • Uninstalling the App and deleting the corresponding App Data folder will permanently remove all locally collected information.
  • For remote sync, you can delete the data directly from your private GitHub repository at any time.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and/or providing a notice within the App.
